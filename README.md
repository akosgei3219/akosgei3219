### About the altitude

Santa Fe sits above 7,000 feet. If you're flying in from sea level, that number will matter more than your goal pace.

Drink more water than feels reasonable, and start days before you get here. Come in early if your schedule allows it. Go out slower than the plan says — you can always spend it later.

The hills are honest. The air is thin. You'll be fine.
